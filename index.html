Our story

reading

Once upon a time, a family from a big, noisy city dreamed about a small house in Texas. They wanted a wide porch with two old rocking chairs, a warm fireplace in the living room and a garden with a white fence. Every evening they looked at photos of old farmhouses on the internet and said to each other: “One day we will live there.”

After many years, they found an old farmhouse near Austin. The house was old and tired — the porch was broken, the windows were dirty and the roof needed new metal sheets — but it had a beautiful golden star on the gable and an old windmill in the garden. The family loved it from the first minute. “This is our dream house,” they said, and they bought it the same week.

They worked all summer. The mother painted the walls a warm cream colour, the father fixed the roof and the children planted flowers and a small vegetable garden. They built a new two-storey porch with wooden posts, two rocking chairs and a longhorn skull above the door. In autumn the house was finally ready, and the whole family sat on the porch, drank iced tea and watched the Texas sunset.

Today their dream house is warm and full of life. In the morning they drink coffee on the porch and watch the sun rise over the fields. In the evening they sit by the fireplace, tell long stories and listen to the wind. The old windmill turns slowly, the golden star shines on the roof, and everybody in the family says the same words: “This is our dream house — our Texas dream house.”

— The end. Or maybe the beginning. 🌅
